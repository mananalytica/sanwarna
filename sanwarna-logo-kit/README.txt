SANWARNA logo kit

vector/   SVG files. Scale to any size without blurring. Use these for
          print, packaging and anything a designer or printer asks for.
png/      The same logos as images with a transparent background.
social/   Ready-sized images for profile pictures and banners.
website/  Icon and link-preview files (already installed on the site).

Versions
  black-gold   black logo, gold sparkle   -> on white or light backgrounds
  white-gold   white logo, gold sparkle   -> on black or dark backgrounds
  black/white  one colour                 -> stamps, embossing, engraving
  gold         all gold                   -> foil printing

Shapes
  mark         the S on its own
  stacked      the S above the name
  horizontal   the S beside the name

Colours
  Black  #1D1D1F     Off-white  #FBFBFD
  Gold   #B8874E (on light)   #D9AE72 (on dark)

Lettering: Jost Light (free, open licence), converted to shapes, so no
font is needed to open or print these files.
